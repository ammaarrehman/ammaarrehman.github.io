A quick intro.

I'm finishing my A.A. at Howard Community College this December on a 3.65 GPA, Dean's List, and I start at the University of Maryland's Smith School of Business in January 2027 to study Information Systems.

This fall I joined Cyber Ready Clinic's analyst cohort. Working alongside senior practitioners, I help assess nonprofit clients' cyber readiness with the Discover5 and Ready5 frameworks and map the gaps we find to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and CIS Controls.

A Prometheus/Grafana stack handles monitoring, and everything I build gets written up on GitHub, this site included.
