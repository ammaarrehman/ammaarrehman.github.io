trading-lab

              
Do popular trading strategies actually have an edge? Nine experiments: stock mean reversion, a crypto trend filter, Kalshi 15-minute markets, dual momentum, copying Congress, a holiday effect, a Kalshi model search, buying the close and selling the open, and a published trend-following allocation. Pass/fail rules committed to git before any data, backtests built to catch their own bugs, each judged on a single run. All nine rejected, $0 lost.
