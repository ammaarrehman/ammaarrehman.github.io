## Island visual assessment

1. Terrain texture is too busy in broad areas. Checker/dither patterns and rectangular tall-grass groupings compete with characters and landmarks.
2. Buildings, boat, signs and lighthouse largely retain the previous flat drawing style beside newly shaded vegetation and rocks. Outline recoloring alone does not unify them.
3. Paths are smoother but still lack deliberate arrival spaces, connected yards, framing and destination hierarchy. The conversion did not redesign the map.
4. Dense canopies and undergrowth obscure routes. More props are not necessarily better; intentional empty space would help.
5. Night lighting can wash out building details. NPC speech, interaction labels and onboarding can overlap near the player, especially on phones.
6. Painted terrain and gameplay boundaries need to agree before new movement mechanics are added.

Recommendation: propose one cohesive village-to-meadow-to-water slice, with matched building art, quieter ground, clustered vegetation, authored path transitions, readable sightlines and restrained lighting. Show before/after at actual game scale, desktop and phone, day and night. Obtain approval before extending it island-wide. Do not repeat a global texture substitution and call it finished. The Phase 12 map/layout approval remains a separate gate.
